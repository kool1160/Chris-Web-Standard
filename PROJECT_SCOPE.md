## Website boundaries

- Prefer static/content-first delivery until a real requirement needs server-side state.
- Do not add auth, database, CMS, dashboard, customer portal, billing, or app-like workflows unless they are explicitly in scope.
- Preserve approved brand identity; do not redesign incidentally while fixing functionality.
- Forms must validate, fail clearly, and protect against obvious abuse.
- Third-party scripts/tracking require a stated business purpose and privacy review.
- Production domain/DNS changes are controlled actions.

## Scope-change rule

Only the Review-Control Chat may change this file after an owner business/product decision. Codex may report a conflict but cannot expand the site to resolve it.

New ideas go to `BACKLOG.md` until deliberately promoted.
